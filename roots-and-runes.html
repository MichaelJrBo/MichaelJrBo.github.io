Design decisions

Divine light beam attack

Problem
Unity's built-in particle system felt too flat for a big, high-impact attack.
What I did
Built the beam from a Line Renderer with additive blending, a warning circle on the ground before it strikes, a 2D point light that lights up nearby enemies and terrain, plus a short screen flash and camera shake.
Why
The warning circle tells the player where the beam will land, and the light, flash and shake make the hit feel powerful.
